## ARTICLE 1 — FORMATION AND NAME

1.1 **Formation.** The Member forms a limited liability company (the "Company") by filing Articles of Organization with the State of Michigan (LARA) under the Michigan Limited Liability Company Act, 1993 PA 23, MCL 450.4101 et seq.

1.2 **Name.** The name of the Company is **Phillias Federal LLC**.

1.3 **Registered Office and Resident Agent.** The registered office and resident agent are as stated in the Articles of Organization, as amended from time to time.

1.4 **Term.** The Company is perpetual unless dissolved under Article 10.

## ARTICLE 2 — PURPOSE

2.1 The purpose of the Company is to engage in any lawful act or activity for which a limited liability company may be organized under Michigan law, including without limitation:
   (a) Applying for and performing contracts, grants, and cooperative agreements with the United States government, including SBIR, STTR, and Other Transaction (OT) awards and prize competitions;
   (b) Research, development, engineering, software, and product design services;
   (c) AI-native design-to-manufacturing services, including CAD drafting and coordination of outsourced fabrication;
   (d) Any other lawful business purpose consistent with the Articles of Organization.

3.2 **Additional Contributions.** No Member is required to make additional capital contributions. Additional contributions may be made only with the Member's written consent and shall be credited to capital accounts.

## ARTICLE 4 — MANAGEMENT

4.1 **Member-Managed.** The Company is member-managed. The sole Member has the full power and authority to manage and bind the Company, including signing contracts, opening bank accounts, and executing filings.

4.2 **Officers/Managers.** The Member may appoint officers or managers by written consent, with such titles and authority as the Member designates.

4.3 **Delegation.** The Member may delegate any authority to agents, employees, contractors, or attorneys-in-fact of the Company, including authority to execute federal proposal documents on behalf of the Company.

4.4 **US Ownership Requirement.** The Member represents that the Company is at all times owned and controlled by United States citizens or permanent resident aliens, as required for Small Business Concern (SBC) eligibility under the SBIR/STTR programs, and that no foreign ownership, control, or influence (FOCI) exists that would disqualify the Company under applicable federal regulations.

4.5 **Authorized Signatory for Federal Awards.** The Member, or any person the Member designates in writing, may act as the Company's Corporate Official / Authorized Organizational Representative for purposes of certifying and submitting proposals and accepting awards.

## ARTICLE 5 — PROFITS, LOSSES, AND DISTRIBUTIONS

5.1 **Allocation.** All profits and losses are allocated 100% to the Member.

5.2 **Distributions.** Distributions shall be made to the Member at the Member's discretion, subject to applicable law and the Company's obligations.

5.3 **Taxes.** The Company is a disregarded entity for federal income tax purposes. The Member reports the Company's income and losses on the Member's personal return.

## ARTICLE 6 — BOOKS, RECORDS, AND FISCAL YEAR

6.1 **Books.** The Company shall maintain complete books of account, including separate records for any federal awards (awards, expenditures, cost accounting) sufficient to satisfy FAR/2 CFR and audit requirements.

6.2 **Fiscal Year.** Calendar year (January 1 – December 31).

6.3 **Inspection.** The Member may inspect all books and records at any time.

## ARTICLE 7 — TRANSFER OF MEMBERSHIP INTEREST

7.1 **Consent Required.** A Member may not transfer, assign, or pledge any part of its interest without the written consent of the Member (which, being the sole Member, acts in its sole discretion).

7.2 **Permitted Transferee Conditions.** Any permitted transferee must meet the US-ownership requirement in §4.4 to retain SBC eligibility.

## ARTICLE 8 — INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 The Company shall indemnify and hold harmless the Member and any manager, officer, employee, or agent to the fullest extent permitted by law against liabilities incurred in good faith in the Company's business, subject to applicable law and insurance.

## ARTICLE 9 — INSURANCE

9.1 The Company may obtain and maintain insurance as the Member determines appropriate, including general liability, professional liability, and cyber insurance.

## ARTICLE 10 — DISSOLUTION

10.1 The Company shall dissolve upon the earliest of: (a) written consent of the Member; (b) the event of dissolution set forth in the Articles of Organization or Act 23 of 1993; or (c) a final decree of judicial dissolution.

10.2 Upon dissolution, assets are applied to creditors and then distributed to the Member.

## ARTICLE 11 — MISCELLANEOUS

11.1 **Amendments.** This Agreement may be amended by written instrument signed by the Member.

11.2 **Governing Law.** Michigan law (Act 23 of 1993) governs this Agreement.

11.3 **Severability.** Any unenforceable provision is severed; the remainder stays in effect.

11.4 **Binding Effect.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Member and the Member's heirs, successors, and permitted assigns.
