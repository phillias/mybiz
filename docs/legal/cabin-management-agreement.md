## 1. PURPOSE AND GRANT

1.1 Owner owns the Property. Operator desires to market and operate the Property as short-term vacation rental lodging on one or more booking platforms (including Airbnb and Hipcamp, under the brands "Crooked River Log Cabin" and "The Rusty Rail Cabin"), and Owner desires to engage Operator to do so.

1.2 Owner grants Operator a non-exclusive right to market, advertise, list, book, collect payments for, and operate the Property as short-term rental lodging, subject to this Agreement. **This is a management arrangement, not a lease or tenancy.** Owner retains title, possession (subject to guest stays), and control of the Property.

1.3 Nothing in this Agreement transfers any real property interest.

## 2. TERM AND TERMINATION

2.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated by either party on 30 days' written notice, or immediately by Owner for Operator's material breach (with cure opportunity) or by Operator for Owner's material breach.

2.2 Upon termination, Operator shall promptly remit all outstanding revenue owed to Owner, transfer or close Operator-held platform accounts for the Property (or remove the Property listings) as Owner directs, and return keys/access codes.

## 3. OPERATOR'S DUTIES

3.1 Create, maintain, and operate Property listings on booking platforms; set pricing within Owner-approved parameters; manage calendars, bookings, guest communications, check-in/check-out, and reviews.

3.2 Arrange and pay for cleaning and turnover services, restocking of supplies, and routine housekeeping.

3.3 Perform or arrange minor maintenance and repairs up to $[___] per occurrence (or other agreed threshold); items above the threshold require Owner's prior written approval (email acceptable).

3.4 **Taxes.** Collect and remit all applicable taxes on guest stays in compliance with law, including Michigan 6% use tax and any county/village lodging or occupancy taxes, and maintain records. Where a platform (e.g., Airbnb) collects and remits automatically, Operator shall verify and document that remittance.

3.5 **Compliance.** Operate the Property in compliance with all applicable federal, state, and local laws, ordinances, zoning, and platform rules (including any short-term rental registration or permit requirements).

3.6 Maintain the Property in a clean, habitable condition and handle guest complaints.

3.7 Notify Owner promptly of any incident, damage, guest injury, or claim.

## 4. OWNER'S DUTIES

4.1 Maintain title to the Property and deliver it in a condition suitable for short-term rental.

4.2 Maintain and pay for the Property's underlying insurance (homeowners/property coverage) and keep the Operator named as an **additional insured** on that policy (at the Operator's request, with reasonable documentation).

4.3 Approve Operator's structural or capital improvements and repairs above the threshold in §3.3; Operator has no authority to make capital improvements without approval.

4.4 Cooperate with Operator's compliance obligations (permits, inspections, registrations) and the Operator's insurance procurement.

4.5 Provide access and access codes, and disclose all material facts about the Property (well/septic, known hazards, restrictions) affecting short-term rental use.

## 5. REVENUE, FEES, AND EXPENSES

5.1 **Gross Booking Revenue** means all guest payments for stays at the Property, before platform commissions/fees, cleaning fees, and taxes.

5.2 Operator shall collect all Gross Booking Revenue through platform accounts it controls.

5.3 **Operator's Management Fee:** [__]% of Gross Booking Revenue.

5.4 **Reimbursable Operator Expenses** (paid from Gross Booking Revenue before remittance): platform commissions/fees, cleaning and turnover costs, supplies/consumables, taxes, and other directly attributable costs Operator incurs and documents.

5.5 **Remittance to Owner:** Gross Booking Revenue − platform fees − taxes − reimbursable expenses − Operator's management fee = net remittance, payable to Owner within [15] days after the end of each calendar month, with a monthly statement itemizing bookings, fees, expenses, and the calculation.

5.6 Operator shall keep the remittance formula transparent and auditable; Owner may inspect Operator's booking and expense records on reasonable notice.

## 6. INSURANCE AND LIABILITY

6.1 Operator shall obtain and maintain commercial hospitality/general liability insurance for its operation of the Property, naming the Owner as an additional insured, with limits of at least $[1,000,000] per occurrence.

6.2 Owner shall maintain property/liability coverage on the real estate and keep Operator as an additional insured to the extent reasonably available.

6.3 Each party is liable for its own negligence; neither is liable for the other's acts except as provided by law.

6.4 This Agreement does not create a partnership, joint venture, or employment relationship. Operator is an independent contractor.

## 7. INDEMNIFICATION

7.1 Operator indemnifies and holds Owner harmless from claims arising out of Operator's operation of the Property (guest claims, Operator's negligence) to the extent covered by or exceeding Operator's insurance, except claims caused by Owner's negligence.

7.2 Owner indemnifies and holds Operator harmless from claims arising out of Owner's negligence or undisclosed defects, except claims caused by Operator's negligence.

## 8. GUESTS

8.1 Operator may establish house rules (noise, pets, smoking, occupancy limits, quiet hours) consistent with local law and shall communicate them to guests.

8.2 Owner may set reasonable occupancy limits and amenity standards; Operator shall enforce them.
