## ARTICLE 1 — FORMATION AND NAME

1.1 **Formation.** The Member forms a limited liability company (the "Company") by filing Articles of Organization with the State of Michigan (LARA) under the Michigan Limited Liability Company Act, 1993 PA 23, MCL 450.4101 et seq.

1.2 **Name.** The name of the Company is **White Pines Lodging LLC**.

1.3 **Registered Office and Resident Agent.** As stated in the Articles of Organization, as amended.

1.4 **Term.** Perpetual unless dissolved under Article 10.

## ARTICLE 2 — PURPOSE

2.1 The purpose of the Company is to engage in any lawful act or activity for which a limited liability company may be organized under Michigan law, including without limitation:
   (a) Owning, operating, and managing short-term rental lodging accommodations;
   (b) Operating the property at 2763 Powers Rd, Alanson, MI 49706 as a short-term rental under the **Airbnb** and **Hipcamp** brands "Crooked River Log Cabin" and "The Rusty Rail Cabin," together with such other brands and platforms as the Member designates;
   (c) Entering into and performing the Cabin Management Agreement with the property Owner (as defined therein);
   (d) Any other lawful business purpose.

## ARTICLE 4 — MANAGEMENT

4.1 **Member-Managed.** The Company is member-managed; the sole Member has full power and authority to manage and bind the Company.

4.2 **Delegation.** The Member may delegate authority to agents, employees, contractors, or attorneys-in-fact, including authority to operate the Company's platform accounts (Airbnb, Hipcamp) and to sign the Cabin Management Agreement.

## ARTICLE 5 — PROFITS, LOSSES, AND DISTRIBUTIONS

5.1 **Allocation.** All profits and losses allocated 100% to the Member.

5.2 **Distributions.** At the Member's discretion, subject to law and obligations.

5.3 **Taxes.** Disregarded entity; income/losses reported on the Member's personal return.

## ARTICLE 6 — BOOKS, RECORDS, AND FISCAL YEAR

6.1 **Books.** Complete books of account, including per-brand and per-platform revenue records sufficient to support the tax and remittance obligations under the Cabin Management Agreement (Michigan use tax, county lodging tax, Owner remittances).

6.2 **Fiscal Year.** Calendar year.

## ARTICLE 7 — TRANSFER OF MEMBERSHIP INTEREST

7.1 **Consent Required.** No transfer, assignment, or pledge without written consent of the Member.

## ARTICLE 8 — INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 To the fullest extent permitted by law for liabilities incurred in good faith, subject to insurance.

## ARTICLE 9 — INSURANCE

9.1 The Company shall obtain and maintain commercial hospitality liability insurance covering its operation of the property, including naming the property Owner as an additional insured as required by the Cabin Management Agreement.

## ARTICLE 10 — DISSOLUTION

10.1 Upon the earlier of: written consent of the Member; an event of dissolution under Act 23 of 1993 or the Articles; or final judicial decree.

10.2 Assets applied to creditors, then distributed to the Member.

## ARTICLE 11 — MISCELLANEOUS

11.1 **Amendments.** By written instrument signed by the Member.
11.2 **Governing Law.** Michigan (Act 23 of 1993).
11.3 **Severability.**
11.4 **Binding Effect.** Binds and benefits the Member and heirs, successors, and permitted assigns.
