## 7. House rules + logistics

**Check-in/out:** self check-in after 4 pm via smart lock (Schlage Encode / Yale Assure / August — programmable codes, no app requirement); checkout 11 am. Unique code per booking (4 pm day-of → 11 am checkout); master code + keyed backup in coded lockbox for plow/cleaner; battery-alert routine.

**Max guests:** [6]. Enforce no unregistered guests (protects occupancy-tax + insurance position).

**Pets:** dogs welcome — $50, 2-dog max, no beds/furniture, never left alone unless crated, clean up the yard, leash on neighbors' property. Cats by request.

**Smoking:** none inside; outdoors 25 ft from doors; MJ outside only.

**Quiet hours:** 10 pm–8 am; no amplified music; no parties (loud lake-season parties = #1 STR complaint vector — say it plainly).

**Fire:** pit use only, never unattended, no fireworks; fireplace per house manual.

**Winter:** shoes off at door (boot rack); don't block the plow lane; thermostat rules to prevent frozen pipes.

**Cancellation (recommended):** **Strict** — full refund ≥30 days, 50% 7–30 days, none <7 days. Sweet spot for a seasonal destination with long lead times. (Optionally Moderate in Nov/April shoulder.)

**Logistics to note in listing:** quiet road (deer, farm equipment), no street parking, nearest 24-hr emergency + grocery in Petoskey, cell varies but WiFi calling works.
