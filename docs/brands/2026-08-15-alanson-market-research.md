# 2763 Powers Rd, Alanson MI — Market Research Digest

Created: 2026-08-15
Source: two parallel research subagents (Airbnb market + Hipcamp market), all findings live-verified 2026-08-15 unless flagged.

## Property context

- Log cabin at 2763 Powers Rd, Alanson MI 49706 (Emmet County, northern lower peninsula).
- Located in **Little Traverse Township** (not Alanson Village proper) — verify zoning with that township's administrator.
- Alanson sits on the Crooked River between Crooked Lake and Burt Lake; the 38-mi **Inland Waterway** passes through; the **North Western State Trail** rail-trail runs through Alanson via Powers Rd; **Pellston Regional (PLN)** is the closest airport (~15 min).
- Not waterfront, on a rural road → price below Petoskey/Harbor Springs waterfront, above deep-rural cabins.

## Seasonality (both agents agree)

| Season | Months | Occupancy | ADR / price band | Drivers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Summer peak | Late Jun–Aug | 70–75% | Highest | Families, boaters, Petoskey stone hunting, Blissfest (mid-Jul), Ugotta Regatta |
| Fall color | Sep–mid Oct | 45–55% | Moderate-high | Tunnel of Trees (M-119), Balloons Over Bay Harbor, wineries |
| Winter ski | Dec–Mar | 35–45% | Elevated weekends | Nub's Nob / Boyne Highlands / Boyne Mountain, snowmobile trails, holiday 2–3× |
| Spring / late fall | Apr–May, Nov | 20–30% | Lowest | Trough; hunting season Nov |

- Booking lead time ~92 days median; ~60% of revenue from ~10 summer weeks + 4–5 winter holiday weeks. November/April are troughs.
- 2–3 BR non-waterfront log cabin comps: **$150–$250/night summer**, **$120–$170 shoulder**, **$180–$260 winter ski weekends**; cleaning fee $100–$150. Hipcamp cabin comps: **$120–$220** (rustic–modest), tent sites $40–$75, RV $50–$90.

## Guest segments & conversion cues

Summer families (lake access, yard, bike trail), anglers/boaters (Inland Waterway, launches), ski families (fireplace, plowed drive, ski rack), fall-color couples (Tunnel of Trees, wineries), snowmobilers (trail access, trailer parking), remote workers (fast WiFi, workspace). Repeatedly-mentioned differentiators in reviews: **hot tub, fire pit, trail access, Petoskey stone hunting, water/dock access, walkable downtown**. EV chargers are a rare differentiator.

## Regulatory (Emmet County / Alanson)

| Item | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Village of Alanson STR ordinance | **None** (2026) | General zoning only |
| Emmet County STR chapter | **None** in County Zoning Ordinance | Verify township zoning (Little Traverse Twp) for "tourist home"/"cabin court" definitions |
| City of Petoskey | Ordinance No. 798 (Jan 2025), annual license, 40-license cap | Not applicable (different jurisdiction) |
| City of Harbor Springs | Chapter 79A, 61-cap, natural-person licensee only | Not applicable |
| **MI 6% use tax** | **Mandatory** — stays <30 days | **Airbnb auto-collects/remits; Hipcamp/Vrbo/direct do NOT** — host must register with MI Treasury + file |
| Emmet County 5% local accommodation tax | Per ROAM — verify with county treasurer | Not collected by platforms typically |
| Health dept (well/septic) | Likely required for tourist accommodation | Emmet County Health Dept; EGLE "camping cabin" = <400 sq ft, on skids, no direct water — check if cabin exceeds this |
| HOA / deed covenants | **Critical to verify** | Lake/river communities often restrict rentals |

Verify: township zoning admin, deed/HOA, MI use-tax registration, county lodging tax, health dept well/septic, insurance (commercial hospitality policy, not just homeowners).
